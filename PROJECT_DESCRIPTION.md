STUPID GIFTS : Thoughtful Gifts Are Overrated
Built for the Stupid Ideas Hackathon, STUPID GIFTS is a hilariously unhelpful gift recommendation app. Enter who you're buying for and their most ridiculous quirk, and the app recommends a real, purchasable gift that's perfectly terrible for them.
Each recommendation comes with a picture, description, fictional five-star review, purchase link, and sharing feature, because bad decisions are best when shared.
